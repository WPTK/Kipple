Feeds that can't be fetched are easier to repair: the feed's empty list and its Feed health row give the reason in plain words, when it last worked and when Kipple tries again, with Edit address and Try now buttons, and Edit feed opens with the same reason and the address field focused (the server's own error text sits behind Details on Feed health). Edit feed is grouped into Basics, This feed's schedule and On this device, every "use the default" choice reads "Same as Settings (every 30 minutes)" with its real value, Save is always "Save" and stays off until something changes, and the off switch is "Pause this feed". Delete dialogs say how many articles go and that it can't be undone, and name it on the button ("Delete feed and 312 articles"). Add feed shows "Looking for a feed at..." while it works, keeps Title and Folder under Options, and offers the own-network option as soon as a private-looking address is typed; the empty library's "Add your first feed" opens it in place. Feed health has a one-line summary that doubles as the filter ("1 feed needs attention. 10 working.") and "Update list" in the header.
