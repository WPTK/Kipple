Web sign-in no longer refuses the right password after ten wrong ones: wrong passwords are now slowed per client (five free, then a wait that doubles from two seconds to a minute, cleared by a correct password and forgotten after an hour with no failure), and the client is told apart by one resolver that believes `X-Forwarded-For` (the rightmost hop that is not a listed proxy) or `CF-Connecting-IP` only from a peer in `KIPPLE_TRUSTED_PROXY_IPS`. With that list correct, every visitor is a separate client and a stranger cannot affect your sign-in; only people who truly share one address (an unlisted proxy, Docker Desktop's gateway, carrier NAT) share a budget, and a flood from them can make sign-in answer "busy, try again" (503), never a lockout.
