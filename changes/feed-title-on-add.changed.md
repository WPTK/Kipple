A feed you add without a title is named with the title the feed gives itself as soon as its first fetch finishes, and the web app shows that name right away instead of the site's address. The title is cleaned up on the way in (a title escaped twice or written over several lines reads as plain text, and a very long one is cut to 200 characters). A title you give the feed, now or later, always wins. Feeds imported from an OPML file without a title start out named after their site like any other new feed. The Add feed dialog says when the title will be filled in from the feed. (#255)
