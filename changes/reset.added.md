Reset Kipple and start over: Settings > Account & Devices > Reset Kipple erases the feeds, folders, history, settings and account and returns Kipple to setup, where you create a new account or restore a backup. It asks for your password and for you to type `reset kipple`, keeps a safety copy of the old database in `backup/pre-restore-*` (restorable with `kipple restore`), and restarts through your restart policy while the page waits. If your compose file or `.env` sets `KIPPLE_USERNAME` and `KIPPLE_PASSWORD`, the dialog warns that Kipple would create that account again, and can ignore them until a new account exists. (#283)
