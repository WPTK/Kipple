The list options menu on a phone no longer covers the bottom tab bar and the home-indicator area: it stops above them, scrolls when it is taller than the space left (in landscape too), and shows a scrollbar so you can tell. Its labels are consistent: the Email - Compact layout is now called Headlines, the Gazette and Inbox descriptions no longer repeat the Order section or each other, "Use device default" and "Use the standard view" no longer sit side by side, and on a feed or folder the heading says the choices are for that feed on this device.
