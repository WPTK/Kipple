Keyboard: the single-key shortcuts (j and k, s, m, o, x, z, c, r, the g chords, the bracket and brace keys, Shift+A, / and ?), the shortcuts overlay and the Settings option for them are gone. Every action has a visible control, so nothing depends on remembering a key. Tab, the arrow keys between rows and in the sidebar, Enter and Space, and Esc to close a menu or dialog or to leave an open article all work as before.
