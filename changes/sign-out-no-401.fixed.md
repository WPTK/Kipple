Signing out sends nothing that needs the session it is ending (the reading data, queued changes, other requests), so the browser console logs no 401 error, and the sign-in screen shows only once this device's offline copies are deleted, even when a request already on its way answers first. Changes made offline that wait to be sent are kept if the sign-out does not go through, and sent then. A sign-out that gets no answer within 10 seconds is given up and says so, and a sign-out of a session that had already ended still signs the browser out. (#549)
