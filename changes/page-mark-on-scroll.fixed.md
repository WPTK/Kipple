Gazette: with mark as read while scrolling, a story you scroll past is marked read even when the page is laid out again at that moment (taller stories in another column, a new plan, a text size change), and a story the page moves above the top without a scroll is never marked. When the page changes above the screen (smaller or larger text, a failed picture, a removed story, the note about failing feeds), the story at the top stays where it was once you stop scrolling, and at the very top of the list you see what appears there.
