Keyboard: the article list is one Tab stop. Tab enters it on the selected article (or the first one) and the next Tab presses leave it for the controls after it, such as the open article's toolbar. The Down and Up arrow keys move between articles when one has focus, as j and k do, and also work with single-key shortcuts off. Before, every article was its own Tab stop and the list loads more articles as focus moves down, so in a library of any size Tab never got past the list. (#369)
