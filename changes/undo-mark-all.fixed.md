Undo now always applies to your most recent change, even when you press `z` or Undo before the server has confirmed that change (Shift+A, mark above or below, or a single mark or star): it waits for that change, showing "Undoing…", and then undoes it. If the change turned out to do nothing, Undo says "Nothing to undo" and leaves earlier changes alone. Before, Undo pressed during that wait could reverse an older change and leave the new one in place.
