List keyboard: the row that has focus is the row x, m, s, o, { and } and j and k act on, however focus got there (Tab, the arrow keys, or a click on the row or on one of its buttons); before, they acted on a separate selection and did nothing, with no sign, until you pressed j or k. In Cards the focus ring now follows the card's rounded corners; Shift+Tab from the reader toolbar lands on the open article after you step through articles with its Next and Previous buttons. With an article open beside the list, those keys act on that article whenever focus is outside the list (in the reader), and the open row is marked (a bar on its edge) apart from the row the cursor is on.
