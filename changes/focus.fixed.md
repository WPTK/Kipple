Keyboard focus stays where you are: Next article, Previous article, Full text, Refresh all feeds and Oldest first keep focus on the button you pressed (beside the list, the article that arrives is announced); Enter or Space on a feed or folder in the sidebar opens it with focus still on it; the Edit feed sheet opens on itself, with Tab reaching its first field before the buttons. In the article list, Home, End, PageUp and PageDown move focus between rows and Space opens the focused row; after Next, Shift+Tab back into the list lands on the open article; a focused card's ring follows its rounded corners. ArrowDown in the search field moves into the results, Home and End pick the first and last choice of a segmented setting, and the Reading appearance panel fits the window.
