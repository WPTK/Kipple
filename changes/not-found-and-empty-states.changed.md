Empty, missing and failed screens share one shape (what this is, why, and a next step): a missing page, feed, folder, article, list or settings page says "<Thing> not found" with its own tab title and a way out instead of quietly showing Unread, an empty folder says it has no feeds, a library with no feeds shows one welcome (the reader pane, sidebar and Search no longer repeat it), an article that is only a title says so and offers the original, and each screen announces its own loading.
